# Slice – New User Onboarding Product Improvement Case Study

A product management case study focused on identifying friction points in Slice's new-user onboarding journey and proposing practical, user-centered improvements.

## 📌 Project Overview

The goal of this case study was to analyze the onboarding experience from a new user's perspective, identify key pain points, prioritize product opportunities, and design improved onboarding interfaces.

The project combines:

- User journey mapping
- Problem identification
- RICE prioritization
- Product solution design
- Figma prototyping
- Success metrics definition

---

## 🔍 Problem

New users may experience friction during onboarding due to:

- Too many details required during signup
- Verification taking too long
- Difficulty finding important features
- Too many options and features
- Unclear guidance on what to do next

These issues can create confusion and reduce the likelihood of users completing their onboarding journey.

---

## 🧭 User Journey Map

The onboarding journey was analyzed across six stages:

**Discover → Sign Up → Verification → Explore → Navigate → First Action**

The journey map captures the user's actions, experience, pain points, and opportunities for improvement at each stage.

## 🎯 Key Pain Points

### 1. Signup Friction
Too many details are required during registration.

### 2. Verification Delay
Users may become frustrated when verification takes too long.

### 3. Navigation Difficulty
Users can struggle to find important features.

### 4. Feature Overload
Too many options can overwhelm new users.

### 5. Unclear Next Steps
Users may not know what action to take after completing a step.

---

## 📊 RICE Prioritization

I used the **RICE framework** to prioritize potential product improvements based on:

- Reach
- Impact
- Confidence
- Effort

### RICE Formula

**RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort**

The prioritization helped identify which improvements could provide the highest potential value relative to implementation effort.

The detailed RICE analysis was completed in Excel.

📊 **[View RICE Prioritization Excel File](RICE-Prioritization.xlsx)**

---

## 💡 Recommended Product Solutions

Based on the prioritization analysis, four key improvements were identified:

### 01 — Simplify Signup

**Problem:** Too many details required.

**Solution:** Introduce a shorter signup form and autofill where possible.

### 02 — Faster Verification

**Problem:** Verification takes too long.

**Solution:** Add a clear progress indicator and improve the verification flow.

### 03 — Simpler Navigation

**Problem:** Users struggle to find features.

**Solution:** Use clearer labels and guided navigation.

### 04 — Clearer Interface

**Problem:** Too many options and features.

**Solution:** Prioritize key features and reduce unnecessary visual complexity.
